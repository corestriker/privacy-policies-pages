3. Processing of Personal Data

3.1 Local Data Storage

All game-related data (such as scores, timestamps, and app settings like volume and vibration preferences) is stored exclusively on your device. No data is transmitted to external servers.

Highway Lines does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services. No user profiles are created and no personal data is analyzed.
